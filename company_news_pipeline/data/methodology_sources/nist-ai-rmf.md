# NIST AI RMF 1.0 — public-source governance note

Source: NIST, *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework  
Published: 26 January 2023. NIST states that the framework is being revised; check the source page before relying on its version status.  
Scope: Voluntary AI-system risk management, not corporate or industry risk evidence.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Use the framework as a reminder to govern the analysis system across its lifecycle. Identify the intended context and affected parties; map system, data, and deployment risks; measure performance and limitations; and manage risks through controls, monitoring, and accountability. Keep human review available for consequential decisions.

For generated business analysis, record source provenance, distinguish retrieved facts from model inference, expose uncertainty, validate the output schema, and preserve a route for correcting weak or harmful recommendations. Do not treat a valid JSON response as proof that the reasoning is correct.

## Limits

This is an original high-level summary, not NIST guidance text or an implementation checklist. The framework is voluntary and its current revision status must be checked against NIST.

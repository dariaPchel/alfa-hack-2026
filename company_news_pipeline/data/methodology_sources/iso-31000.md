# ISO 31000 — public-source methodology note

Source: ISO, *Risk management — Guidelines*, https://committee.iso.org/standard/31000  
Edition: ISO 31000:2018; ISO's catalog reported the edition confirmed in 2023 when this note was prepared.  
Scope: International, general risk-management guidance. The full standard is copyrighted and is not reproduced here; this note summarizes the public catalog description and the design's general risk workflow.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Treat risk management as a connected cycle rather than a one-time label: establish the context, identify an event, analyze its likelihood and consequences, evaluate its significance against objectives, select a response, and monitor whether the response works. Communicate uncertainty and assumptions to decision makers. The analysis should be proportionate to the evidence and business context.

For each news item, distinguish the source's reported fact from the analyst's interpretation. State which company objective or business indicator may be affected, the time horizon, the scenario range, and what evidence could change the estimate. Do not present a scenario estimate as a guaranteed forecast.

## Limits

This note is not the ISO standard or a compliance checklist. It does not establish Russian legal obligations. Use it as a general structuring aid, alongside applicable local requirements and company-specific evidence.

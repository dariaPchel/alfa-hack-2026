# The Orange Book — public-source methodology note

Edition consulted: GOV.UK page updated 29 July 2026.

Scope: UK public-sector risk management; use only as a methodological reference for this prototype, not as a rule applicable to Russian companies.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Make risk analysis part of planning and decisions. Evaluate uncertainty and implications across options, including opportunities as well as threats. Use the best available information, document gaps, assign ownership, and monitor whether controls and actions are effective. A response plan should be realistic about residual risk and should be revised when evidence changes.

For an industry-level enforcement signal, separate a confirmed event from the inference that enforcement is becoming more intense. Require a time window, a relevant industry or regulator, comparable evidence, and a stated confidence level before presenting a trend as a signal.

## Limits

The publication addresses public organizations in the United Kingdom. Its governance structures and legal context do not transfer directly to private Russian businesses.

# COSO Enterprise Risk Management — public overview note

Source: COSO, *Enterprise Risk Management*, https://www.coso.org/enterprise-risk-management  
Scope: General enterprise risk management. This is an original summary of COSO's public overview; the paid framework and copyrighted supporting materials are not reproduced.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Connect risk analysis to strategy and performance. A reported event matters when a plausible path links it to an objective, a business process, a resource, or a performance measure. Keep that path explicit: event → exposure or dependency → operational/financial consequence → indicator and horizon.

Compare response options against the company's objectives and risk tolerance. Include both downside and upside where the evidence supports them. Explain trade-offs, residual risk, monitoring signals, and who should own each action. A recommendation should not imply that risk can be eliminated when it can only be reduced or transferred.

## Limits

This note is not a substitute for the COSO ERM framework. It is a general reasoning aid and should not be represented as a formal COSO assessment or certification.

# Federal Reserve SR 26-2 — scoped model-risk governance note

Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, SR 26-2, *Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management*, https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm  
Issued: 17 April 2026. The letter says it supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8 and is most relevant to Federal Reserve-regulated banking organizations with more than $30 billion in assets.  
Scope: US banking supervision. This is a contextual reference for model governance, not a universal standard for Russian companies.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Use risk-based controls proportionate to model use, complexity, and potential impact. Keep a record of model purpose, assumptions, input quality, limitations, validation evidence, and changes. Independent challenge and monitoring should focus on material use cases and emerging failure patterns. Human decision makers remain responsible for judging whether analysis is fit for purpose.

For this prototype, preserve model/provider configuration, cost and request metadata, source citations, peer basis, validation warnings, and final structured output. Do not describe an unvalidated percentage estimate as a calibrated model result.

## Limits

This note summarizes the public supervisory letter and is not legal or regulatory advice. SR 26-2 applies in its stated US banking context; users must check the current official letter and applicable local requirements.

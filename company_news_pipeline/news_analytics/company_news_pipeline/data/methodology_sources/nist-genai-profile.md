# NIST Generative AI Profile — public-source governance note

Source: NIST, *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile* (NIST AI 600-1), https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence  
Published: 26 July 2024.  
Scope: Cross-sectoral generative-AI risk profile; it informs controls for this analysis pipeline and does not provide company-specific risk facts.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Assess generative-AI risks in the actual use context and lifecycle. Pay particular attention to source quality, unsupported claims, privacy, security, harmful output, evaluation limits, and human oversight. Keep evidence retrieval and model-generated interpretation separable. Review high-impact recommendations and make uncertainty visible to the user.

Monitor failure patterns such as invented citations, unsupported supplier recommendations, copied prompt-injection instructions from web pages, and numeric estimates that lack peer or company evidence. Define a correction and escalation path rather than relying only on prompt instructions.

## Limits

This concise original summary does not reproduce NIST AI 600-1 and is not a certification or complete control set.

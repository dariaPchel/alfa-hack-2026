Scope: Appraisal of UK government proposals using public money. The note borrows the option-comparison structure only; it does not imply that the rules govern private Russian companies.

## Applying the idea in this pipeline

Before recommending an action, state the objective and compare a reasonable set of options, including maintaining the current approach where relevant. For each option record expected benefits, costs, risks, feasibility, dependencies, evidence quality, and uncertainty. Make material supplier or commercial dependencies explicit. Select an option only after explaining the trade-offs and what monitoring would show whether it is working.

Keep scenario estimates traceable to assumptions. Where a numeric business effect is requested but company financial data is missing, show a range, horizon, confidence, and peer-based assumptions rather than a point forecast with false precision.

## Limits

This is an analyst-authored summary, not the Green Book or a formal appraisal. Government public-value concepts may not map directly to a commercial company's objectives.
